How can I find the Golden Gate Bridge?

The Golden Gate Bridge is located on US Highway 101 and connects the northern tip of the San Francisco Peninsula to Marin County. If you are looking for it using a navigation app like Google Maps or Waze, search for the Golden Gate Bridge Welcome Center as your destination, which sits right at the southern entrance. Because parking is extremely limited and fills up rapidly, driving a personal vehicle is highly discouraged. The best ways to find and reach the bridge depend on how you choose to travel.
